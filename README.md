# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional performance.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

This project demonstrates my approach to data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, KPI calculation, data visualization, and business reporting.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Explore monthly sales trends and changes over time.
- Compare performance across regions and customer segments.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Develop actionable recommendations based on the findings.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and functions
- Data cleaning and validation
- Charts and data visualization
- Interactive dashboards and slicers

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Business Understanding

Defined key business questions to guide the analysis, focusing on revenue, profitability, product performance, regional differences, and discount effectiveness.

### 2. Data Inspection

- Reviewed the dataset structure and column definitions.
- Examined data types, date ranges, and numerical values.
- Identified missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent labels.

### 3. Data Cleaning

- Identified and handled duplicate transactions.
- Investigated missing values.
- Standardized inconsistent text values.
- Validated sales calculations and checked unusual values.
- Preserved the original dataset for reference.

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyzed the dataset across several dimensions:

- **Time:** Monthly sales and profit trends.
- **Products:** Sales and profitability by category and subcategory.
- **Regions:** Regional sales and profit comparisons.
- **Customers:** Performance by customer segment.
- **Discounts:** Discount levels and their relationship with profitability.

### 5. KPI Analysis

Key performance indicators include:

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Measure overall revenue |
| Total Profit | Evaluate profitability |
| Profit Margin | Measure profit relative to sales |
| Total Orders | Understand transaction volume |
| Units Sold | Measure product demand |
| Average Order Value | Measure average sales value per order |

### 6. Data Visualization

Selected charts based on the questions being investigated:

- Line chart for monthly sales trends.
- Bar charts for product and regional comparisons.
- KPI cards for overall business performance.
- Discount-versus-profit analysis to explore profitability patterns.

### 7. Business Recommendations

The final stage translates analytical findings into practical recommendations supported by the data.

Recommendations may focus on product strategy, regional performance, pricing, discount policies, and sales opportunities.
